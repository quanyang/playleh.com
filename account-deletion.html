Delete your MahjongLeh account

You can submit and complete a deletion request here without reinstalling the app. Sign in with the same provider and provider account linked to MahjongLeh so we can verify ownership.

1

Verify the account

Choose the Apple or Google account you previously linked in MahjongLeh. Signing in with an unrelated provider may create a new empty account; if that happens, we remove it and do not treat your original account as deleted.

This page keeps the sign-in session only in memory. It does not load Analytics, and it never displays or stores your Firebase ID, provider credential, or authentication token.

2

Review permanent deletion

  • Your Firebase MahjongLeh account will be permanently deleted.
  • Associated account and Analytics data will be submitted for deletion. Some purchase, fraud-prevention, security, webhook, and financial records may be retained where needed for legal, operational, ownership, or recovery obligations.
  • Deleting MahjongLeh does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription, and a new MahjongLeh account does not automatically inherit a retained purchase.

Guest-only or cannot sign in?

An anonymous guest account has no Apple or Google credential that can prove ownership on the web. If your account was guest-only, or provider recovery fails, contact PlayLeh support. Never send a password, Firebase token, Apple credential, Google credential, or store receipt by email.
