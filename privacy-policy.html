Privacy Policy

The scope of this Policy is limited to information and data collected or received through the MahjongLeh application only.

If you choose to use the application, then you agree to the collection and use of information in relation to this policy. The Personal Information that I collect is used for providing and improving the Service. I will not use or share your information with anyone except as described in this Privacy Policy.

Information Collection and Use

Personal Information

You do not need to give us your name, email address or phone number to play. Ordinary single-player games stay on your device. The information we receive comes from ranked leaderboards, online play and notifications, as described below.

Ranked Leaderboards and Your Guest Account

When you have accepted the current Terms of Service and start a ranked game, or start or join an online game, the application creates an anonymous guest account for you with Google Firebase Authentication. The account is a random identifier. It does not contain your name, email address or phone number.

For ranked play, our servers store, linked to that guest account:

  • the hands you finish, including the tiles dealt, the moves played, the house rules and the result, so that we can check that each score is genuine;
  • your scores, awards and leaderboard positions for each day, week and month;
  • the display name you chose in the game;
  • a record of how many hands you recently uploaded, used to limit abuse.

Leaderboards are public. Other players can see your display name, your score, your position and the tiles and score breakdown of your best winning hand for a day, week or month. They are shown with a random public player identifier, never with your guest account identifier or device information. Display names that contain offensive words are partly hidden with asterisks.

Online Multiplayer

When you play online (Quick Match or private rooms), the display name you choose and your in-game actions are sent to our game server and shown to the other players in your game.

If you have a guest account, the application also sends a sign-in token for it to our game server, so that your online hands can be credited to your account on the Multiplayer leaderboards. Our game server then links that account to your device identifier, an anonymous identifier for this installation of the application and the seat you play in each game. We use this link to credit scores to the right player and to stop one player from taking over another player's seat. Other players never see your account, device or installation identifiers.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, for example for turn reminders or for leaderboard alerts when another player overtakes you, our servers store a notification token for your device. Apple Push Notification service or Firebase Cloud Messaging provides this token, and our servers use it only to send you those notifications. You can turn these notifications off in the application or in your device settings.

Deleting Your Ranked Account

You can delete your ranked guest account at any time in the application, under Options. Deleting it permanently removes your scores, hand records, leaderboard entries, public display name, leaderboard alert settings and the guest account itself. It also stops any further scores from being credited to that account. The removal from public leaderboards starts straight away, and the rest finishes in the background, usually within minutes. If you later play ranked or online again, the application creates a new, unrelated guest account. If you cannot use the application to delete your account, contact us at contact@playleh.com.

How Long We Keep Ranked Data

We keep your ranked data for as long as your guest account exists, so that past daily, weekly and monthly leaderboards stay accurate. When you delete your ranked account, we delete this data as described above. We may keep a record that the account was deleted, without any scores or display name, so that it cannot be credited again.

Data Security

All communication between the application and our servers is encrypted in transit (TLS / HTTPS / WSS).

Children’s Privacy

The application does not address anyone under the age of 17. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 17. In the case we discover that a child under 17 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at contact@playleh.com so that we will be able to do necessary actions.
